20 × 20 grid. Follow each arrow through its bends to the exit.
How to play
The board is 20 × 20. Each arrow is a continuous path that may bend. Tap any segment: the whole arrow follows its own route and exits in the direction of its head. Other arrows can block the exit. Clear every arrow to finish. You have three hearts.
